Letter for the boy overseas:

Hello to you, my dear friend Henry,

I hope you're having the time of your life wherever this digital letter finds you. It's a very commendable and challenging thing that you're doing, and I hope you're enjoying every second of it, even if it's chaos at times. I certainly couldn't ship myself overseas like you have, and to be able to make a plan like you have is really awesome. I hope all the people you've met and the different cultures you're getting to embrace are enriching your life thus far.

I'm busy pushing Slow Hang into the stratosphere of DIY booking in Minneapolis. By the time you get back, I have no doubt that Slow Hang will be a 6-figure company, and likely being traded on the NYSE. I'm in it strictly for the cash. Here's hoping I can partner with Livenation, AEG, or some other corporate monstrosity in the music world to help me achieve my goals. The next show I have coming up is Racecourse. I can't remember if we talked about them or not, but they are awesome and definitely worth checking out (if you are able to in the unique part of the world you're currently residing in). Emo-adjacent,slowcore-ish, beautiful and managed by Numero Group. Legit shit. This is more motion in my sinister plan to chip away from First Avenue's business until they give me a salaried job. Once again, I'm all about the money.

I hope your excursions around the Philippines recording drum tunings was a success, if not on-going now. It was awesome getting to see you before you took off, and I'm really stoked to hear more about your adventures and research in the world of music technology. Please hit me up sometime. Would love to hear where you are and how the experience has treated you so far. If I end up in Europe around the time you'll be there, I'll be more than sure to hit you up. We can immerse ourselves in the world of techno in Berlin, or hit the Windmill Brixton in London to catch the next-up in post punk.

Super stoked to hear about your travels. Take care of yourself!

Will Prim
