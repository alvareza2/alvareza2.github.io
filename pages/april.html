Henry,

I hope this letter finds you in a rabbit-hole of musical and cultural exploration so deep you may as well be the subject of a dust-to-digital post. Have you learned to tell a guitar's age by its timbre alone? Have your fingers become attuned to the bronzed thrum of gamelan?

If, while we were at HMI, someone asked me what your first big leap out from the structure of school would be, I'm not sure I would have placed you on a year-long itinerary across the globe looking hard into a textured and shadowy question. Maybe I would have pictured a sailing coach with a promising moonlight side hustle in a Hippo Campus cover band. Maybe I would have had the foresight to imagine some combination of your Caltopo computer skills and your natural ease on a blues lick taking you to the frontier of your interests. Whatever I might have thought then, I'm currently so impressed by your Watson adventure because it's built around some essential Henry zest, but seems to be largely operating well outside your comfort zone. ‘Seems to be’ because, as it turns out, I got no idea what the hell a day in the life actually looks like. Can't wait to hear more!

I'm writing this from the West Building at HMI, caught in a web of memories from our time here. Sometimes, when I'm entering Stuen or looking into the woods, I'm not sure if something happened in the last six days or over half a decade (!) ago. I spend a lot of time thinking about how much laughing we did in Cabin 7. It's quite strange to hear different kids hollering in there late at night with absolutely zero self-awareness, and, if only for a brief moment, to feel a flash of annoyance (apocryphal!). It makes me realize what a short and special window it is to be 16 in a place like this. You're aware enough of your world to fill it with fun and take good care of it, but delightfully self-involved in your devotion to getting a pumpkin in a porta-potty on a freezing night or making an unsanctioned breakfast for the community just to prove you can break rules for the common good. It makes me so happy to think back on all that. And even though I'm walking on the same ground and scouring our cabin of all its old graffiti before the new group settles in for the semester, I don't worry about replacing those memories. They're burned real deep on the hard drive.

I looked up to you a lot while we were at HMI. I still do. I saw how you balanced all the best of teenage things with a good dose of maturity, and I realized I could do the same. I started thinking about leadership, about walking in the adult world, about committing seriously to sidequests. That last one is big. Hanging out with you helped me see the things I've always been about, and our friendship, coupled with all the expansive identity work that just happens at the Institute, really crystalized my identity. Though we haven't spent enough time together since HMI, I have a strong sense we're both following the essentials we ironed out while we were here. And if a few hours in Colorado Springs is a decent measure, we've both changed a lot, probably for the better, since we hammered out Michigan on a cabin porch like it was the only song on earth.

I wish we could have made that July backpacking trip a reality, if only to see what's loosened, tightened, grown, and composted since our epic four months of continuous IRL friendship in the legendary autumn of ‘20. I'm super excited to follow along on your year of music! Just revamped my banjo using a plastic chopstick and a bread knife (she's finally back to a full 5 strings), so you could say I'm parallel playing in solidarity. In other news, Louise's little brother Fletcher is in this semester and he's a bona fide VIBE. Keep me posted on your stops and sights, passport bro! Can't wait to cross paths again, hopefully more frequently than we have in the last six years.

If you're in search of a book rec over the next year, I just had the most fun with Ed Abbey's Monkey Wrench Gang and I'm hoping to teach FUP by Jim Dodge to the students in December because I love it so goddamn much #TenOutOfTen #EnglishAppren.

Happy sidequesting!

GTA (Got to absquatulate),

Asa
